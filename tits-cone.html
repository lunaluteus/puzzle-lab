Coordinates and conventions

Write xi = λ(αi) for λ in the dual of the simple-root space. The closed fundamental chamber C has xi ≥ 0, and the Tits cone is the union of the chambers wC. Crossing wall i of wC takes you to wsiC.

In rank two the axes show the actual dual coordinates. In rank three, each positive ray is cut at xs + xt + xu = 1 and drawn in the Klein disk. The chamber depth limits the drawn gallery. The background shows the entire interior of the cone or slice.
